# Interpretation Amendment Act, 2004

**DISTRIBUTED BY VERITAS**

**VERITAS MAKES EVERY EFFORT TO ENSURE THE PROVISION OF RELIABLE INFORMATION,\
BUT CANNOT TAKE LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR INFORMATION SUPPLIED.**

Act No. 9 of 2004\
Published in the Government *Gazette* 8th July 2005 (General Notice 303/2005)\
Date of commencement: 8th July 2005

**ACT**

> To amend section 9 of the Interpretation Act \[*Chapter 1:01*\].

ENACTED by the President and the Parliament of Zimbabwe.

## Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Interpretation Amendment Act, 2004.

## Section 2 — Amendment of section 9 of Cap. 1:01

Section 9 of the Interpretation Act \[*Chapter 1:01*\] is amended by the repeal of subsection (1) and the substitution of "—

> "(1)  Unless the context otherwise requires, words importing female persons include male persons and juristic persons and words importing male persons include female persons and juristic persons.".

NOTE FOR ASSISTANCE OF READERS\
(*not part of the Act as published*)

Section 9 of the Interpretation Act, as amended by this Act, is as follows (previous wording of section 9(1) given in italics in square brackets:—

**9 Rules as to gender and number**(1)  Unless the context otherwise requires, words importing female persons include male persons and juristic persons and words importing male persons include female persons and juristic persons. \[**Previous wording**:- *Words importing the masculine gender include females*.\]
(2)  Words in the singular include the plural and words in the plural include the singular.
