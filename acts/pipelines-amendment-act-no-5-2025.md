# Pipelines Amendment Act No. 5, 2025

ZIMBABWE

ACT To amend the Pipelines Act [Chapter 13:08] and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. ENACTED by the Parliament and the President of Zimbabwe. 1

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Pipelines Amendment Act, 2025.

2

Amendment of section 2 of Cap. 13:08 Section 2 (Interpretation) of the Pipelines Act (hereinafter called the principal Act) is amended by the deletion of “Minister” and the substitution of “Minister’’ means the Minister responsible for Energy or any other Minister to whom the President may from time to time, assign the administration of this Act.”. 3

Amendment of section 6 of Cap. 13:08 Section 6 (Protection of land used for pipeline) of the principal Act is repealed and substituted with the following— “(1) Subject to subsection (3), no person shall—

(a)

erect any building or structure or plant any tree within the pipeline reserve; or

Insertion of a new section in Cap. 13:08 The principal Act is amended by the insertion after section 9 of the following— “9A Inspections and maintenance

(1) The grantee may authorise any of its employees or agent to inspect and maintain the pipeline reserve for the purposes of this Act and shall furnish such person with an identification document for the purpose of carrying out such function.

(2) An employee or agent of the grantee shall, in addition to any other functions that may be assigned to them by the grantee, be responsible for inspecting pipeline reserve undertakings to ensure the safety of operations and to investigate accidents.

(3) Subject to subsection (7), an employee, agent or a police officer may at all reasonable times enter or gain access to the pipeline servitude—

(a)

which is located within private property; or

(b) where he or she has reasonable cause to suspect an offence against this Act has been committed;

and shall have power to make such examination, inspection and inquiry and do such things as may appear to him or her necessary for ascertaining whether compliance has been made with this Act.

(4) The powers of entry and inspection conferred by this section shall only be exercised with the consent of the person in charge of the private

property concerned, unless there are reasonable grounds for believing that it is necessary to exercise them for the prevention, investigation or detection of an offence in terms of this Act or for the obtaining of evidence relating to such an offence or for the prevention or curtailment of conditions that may have an adverse effect on the pipeline.

(5) An employee, agent of the grantee or police officer may, in the exercise of the powers conferred upon him or her by this section, seize any equipment or apparatus which he or she has reasonable cause to suspect will afford evidence of the grantee of an offence against this Act and may retain it for so long as may be necessary for the purpose of any examination, investigation, trial or inquiry.

(6) An employee or agent of the grantee may, in the exercise of the powers conferred upon him or her by this section, order a person referred to in subsection (5) to cease any activity, pending investigations by the grantee, which is not in accordance with this Act.

(7) An employee or agent of the grantee shall, on demand by any person affected by the exercise of the powers conferred upon him or her by this section, exhibit the identification document issued to him or her in terms of subsection (1).
