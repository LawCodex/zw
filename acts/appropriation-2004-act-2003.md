No. 11

ZIMBABWE

-----------------

**ACT**

> To apply the sum of money for the service of Zimbabwe during the year ending on the 31<sup>st</sup> December, 2004.
>
> ENACTED by the President and the Parliament of Zimbabwe.
>
> **1 Short title**

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (2004) Act, 2003.

**2 Interpretation**In this Act-
“Estimates of Expenditure” means the Estimates of Expenditure for the year ending on the 31<sup>st</sup> December, 2004 (Cmd. R.Z.3, 2003), submitted to and passed by Parliament;
“Vote” means a vote appropriation for the year ending on the 31<sup>st</sup> December, 2004, as specified in the Estimates of Expenditure.
**3 Consolidated Revenue Fund charged with \$ 7 408 410 103 000**

The Consolidated Revenue Fund is hereby charged with such sums of money as may be required for the service of Zimbabwe during the year on the 31<sup>st</sup> December, 2004, not exceeding in aggregate the sum of seven trillion four hundred and eight billion four hundred and ten million one hundred and three thousand dollars.

**4 Application of moneys granted**

Subject to section *five* and subsection (5) of section 24 of the Audit and Exchequer Act \[*Chapter 22:03*\], the moneys appropriated by section *three* shall be applied to the services detailed in the Schedule and more particularly specified in the Estimates of Expenditure.

**5 Power of the Minister of Finance and Economic Development to authorise transfers between votes.**

\(1\) Where, in the case of any moneys appropriated by section *three* in respect of a particular Vote set out in the Schedule which are to be applied in terms of section *four* to any particular service specified in the Estimates of Expenditure, the administration or provision of such a service is assigned to a different Ministry, the Minister of Finance and Economic Development may direct that any portion of the moneys appropriated in respect of the first-mentioned vote be transferred to such other vote as relates to the Ministry to which the administration or provision of the service concerned has been assigned.

\(2\) In the case of moneys appropriated by section *three* in respect of subhead I.D. Unallocated Reserve of Vote 5, the Minister of Finance and Economic Development may direct that any of the moneys so appropriated for any purpose detailed in the notes to the subhead be transferred to the Vote of any other Ministry for the purpose concerned, and any moneys so transferred shall not be applied for any other purpose:

Provided that, if moneys so transferred are not required for the purpose concerned, the Minister of Finance and Economic Development may direct the transfer of the moneys back to subhead I.D. Unallocated Reserve of Vote 5.

\(3\) In the case of moneys appropriated in section *three* in respect of Vote 6, the Minister of Finance and Economic Development may direct that any portion of the moneys so appropriated be transferred to the Vote of the Appropriate Ministry that may be concerned with the implementation of any particular donor-funded project for the purpose of meeting expenditure incurred in connection with that project, and any moneys so transferred shall not be applied for any other purpose:

Provided that, if any moneys so transferred are not required for the purpose of the project concerned, the Minister of Finance and Economic Development may direct the transfer of the moneys back to Vote 6.

\(4\) Moneys transferred in terms of subsection (1), (2) or (3) shall be accounted for as if they have been appropriated by section *three* to the Vote to which they are so transferred, and shall be deemed to have been so appropriated.
