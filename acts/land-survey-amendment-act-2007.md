# Land Survey Amendment Act, 2007

**DISTRIBUTED BY VERITAS**

**VERITAS MAKES EVERY EFFORT TO ENSURE THE PROVISION OF RELIABLE INFORMATION,\
BUT CANNOT TAKE LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR INFORMATION SUPPLIED.**

Act No. 3 of 2007.

Published in the Government *Gazette*: Friday 10th August 2007.

Commencement: Friday 10th August 2007.

**LAND SURVEY AMENDMENT ACT, 2007**

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

**ACT**

> To amend sections 2 and 12 of the Land Survey Act \[*Chapter 20:12*\] and sections 2 and 18 of the Land Surveyors Act \[*Chapter 27:06*\]; and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

ENACTED by the President and Parliament of Zimbabwe.

## Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Land Survey Amendment Act, 2007.

## Section 2 — Amendment of section 2 of Cap. 20:12

Section 2 ("Interpretation") of Land Survey Act \[*Chapter 20:12*\] (hereinafter called "the principal Act") is amended by the insertion of the following definition—

> ""specified local qualification" means a degree, diploma or other qualification\
> declared to be a specified local qualification in terms of the Land Surveyors Act \[*Chapter 27:06*\];".
>
> **3 Amendment of section 12 of Cap. 20:12**

Section 12 ("Training leading to recognition") (2) of the principal Act is amended by the repeal of paragraph (a) and the substitution of—

> "(a) in the case of a person who holds a specified local or foreign qualification, the period prescribed by the Board in terms of paragraph (i) of subsection (1) of section *nine*; or".
>
> **4 Amendment of Cap. 27:06**

The Land Surveyors Act \[*Chapter 27:06*\] is amended—

> \(a\) in section 2 ("Interpretation") by the insertion of the following definition—
>
> ""specified local qualification" means a degree, diploma or other qualification declared by the Minister to be a specified local qualification in terms section 18;";
>
> \(b\) in section 18 ("Qualifications for registration as land surveyor-in-training")—
>
> \(i\) by the repeal of subsection (1) and the substitution of—
>
> "(1)  Subject to section 19, any person holding one or more of the following qualifications shall be eligible to apply for registration as a land surveyor-in-training—
>
> \(a\) a specified local qualification;
>
> \(b\) a specified foreign qualification;
>
> \(c\) a specified foreign licence.";
>
> \(ii\) in subsection (2) by the deletion of "paragraphs (b) and (c) of subsection (1)" and the substitution of "subsection (1)".
