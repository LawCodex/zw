# Masvingo State University Amendment Act, 2007

**DISTRIBUTED BY VERITAS**

**VERITAS MAKES EVERY EFFORT TO ENSURE THE PROVISION OF RELIABLE INFORMATION,\
BUT CANNOT TAKE LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR INFORMATION SUPPLIED.**

Act No. 4 of 2007.\
Gazetted: Monday 30th July, 2007.\
Commencement: Monday 30th July, 2007 (date of gazetting).

**MASVINGO STATE UNIVERSITY AMENDMENT ACT, 2007**

**ACT**

> To amend the Masvingo State University Act \[*Chapter 25:24*\].

ENACTED by the President and the Parliament of Zimbabwe.

## Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Masvingo State University Amendment Act, 2007.

## Section 2 — Amendment of long title to Cap. 25:24

The long title to the Masvingo State University Act \[*Chapter 25:24*\] (hereinafter called "the principal Act") is amended by the deletion of "Masvingo State University" and the substitution of "Great Zimbabwe University".

## Section 3 — Amendment of section 2 of Cap. 25:24

Section 2 ("Interpretation") of the principal Act is amended by the repeal of the definition of "University" and the substitution of the following definition—

> ""University" means the Great Zimbabwe University constituted in terms of this Act;".
>
> **4 Amendment of section 3 of Cap. 25:24**

Section 3 ("Establishment of University") (1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of "Masvingo State University" and the substitution of "Great Zimbabwe University".
