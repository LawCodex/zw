# GN 2008-31 - Nominations for Presidential Election

**DISTRIBUTED BY VERITAS TRUST**

**VERITAS MAKES EVERY EFFORT TO ENSURE THE PROVISION OF RELIABLE INFORMATION,\
BUT CANNOT TAKE LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR INFORMATION SUPPLIED.**

General Notice 31 of 2008.

ELECTORAL ACT \[*CHAPTER 2:13*\]

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

Presidential Election 29th March, 2008: Nomination Court Results

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

IT is hereby notified, in terms of section 106 of the Electoral Act \[*Chapter 2:13*\], that at the close of sitting of the Nomination Court held on Friday, 15th February, 2008, the following candidates were duly nominated for election to the office of President:—

*Candidate*

*Party*

Makoni, Herbert Stanley Simba

Independent

Mugabe, Robert Gabriel

ZANU (PF)

Towungana, Langton

Independent

Tsvangirayi, Morgan

MDC

Accordingly polling shall take place on Saturday 29th March, 2008, from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. for the purpose of electing a person to the office of President.

L.C.SEKERAMAYI,

Chief Elections Officer,

26-2-2008 Zimbabwe Electoral Commission.

> This notice was published in a Government *Gazette* Extraordinary dated 26th February 2008.
