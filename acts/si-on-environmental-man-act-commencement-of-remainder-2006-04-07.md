# SI on Environmental Man Act - commencement of remainder 2006-04-07

**DISTRIBUTED BY VERITAS TRUST**

**VERITAS MAKES EVERY EFFORT TO ENSURE THE PROVISION OF RELIABLE INFORMATION,\
BUT CANNOT TAKE LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR INFORMATION SUPPLIED.**

Statutory Instrument 74 of 2006.

**\[CAP. 20:27**

Date of Commencement: Environmental Management Act \[*Chapter 20:27*\]:\
Section 144 and paragraph 1 of Part II of the Sixth Schedule

HIS Excellency the President, in terms of section 1(2) of the Environmental Management Act \[*Chapter 20:27*\], hereby fixes 7th April, 2006, as the date on which section 144 and paragraph 1 of Part II (reference to section 69 of the Water Act \[*Chapter 20:04*\]) of the Sixth Schedule shall come into operation.

*Supplement to the Zimbabwean Government Gazette dated the 24th March, 2006.*
